# Brand files

`sensyble-mark.svg` is **Sensyble's company mark** (the stylised lowercase "y" with a ring), rebuilt
as vectors from the reference artwork. It is Sensyble's trademark, kept here only as the source the
Passyble mark is derived from. Do not use it as the Passyble app icon or ship it on its own.

The Passyble mark is the "y" of the wordmark ("Pass", the lime "y", "ble", as on passyble.com):
Sensyble's "y" turned into a key. The ring (enlarged) is the key's bow, the curved stroke is the
shaft and the short arm is the bit, with a ward slot cut into its end. It stands as in the wordmark:
the bow at the bottom left, the shaft rising to the right, the bit at the top right. There is no
tile or background; the icon is the mark alone on transparent.

| file | what |
|---|---|
| `passyble-icon.svg` | the app icon: the mark in lime `#c6f432` with a darker green border `#4f6b00`, transparent (same as `ui/logo.svg`) |
| `passyble-wordmark-y.svg` | the wordmark's "y": the same two-colour mark in the wordmark's viewBox, for light and dark pages alike |
| `passyble-key-mono.svg` | the bare mark in white (notification / themed icon silhouette) |
| `sensyble-mark.svg` | Sensyble's mark, unrotated and unmodified (reference only, never shipped) |

Colors: lime `#c6f432` (the mark's fill) and darker green `#4f6b00` (its border, about 6% of the
mark's height, at least 1.5 px: 4.8:1 against the lime, 6.1:1 on white, 2.9:1 on the dark
surfaces). Every full-colour mark has the border, in both themes; only the monochrome icons are the
bare mark. See `docs/BRAND.md`, "Logo".

All of these, and every app, tray, installer, extension and Android icon, are generated by
`../make-icons.mjs` (`node desktop-app/scripts/make-icons.mjs` from this repository's root; it also
writes into the sibling Extension and Android repositories; the Mac repository's and the website's
icon scripts import it). Edit the geometry or colors there, not the generated files.
